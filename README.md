# Digital Preservation Sites
This project was created as a part of my Digital Humanities 150 class taught by Professor Benno Herz, which focused on German exiles who migrated to Los Angeles during World War II. In this website, I provide information on two historical homes we visited, belonging to the famous exiles Thomas Mann and Galka Scheyer. I discuss the contributions of these exiles to the literary, film, and art scene of Los Angeles, as well as provide walkable 3D interpretations of rooms from their houses using Three.js. In my essay linked in the site, I discuss the importance of advocacy for the preservation of such historical sites for educational and cultural purposes.
