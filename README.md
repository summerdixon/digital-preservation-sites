# Digital Preservation Sites
This project was created as a part of my Digital Humanities 150 class taught by Professor Benno Herz, which focused on German exiles who migrated to Los Angeles during World War II. In this site, I provide information on two historical sites we visited: the Thomas Mann House and the Galka Scheyer House. I discuss the contributions of these exiles to the film and art scene of Los Angeles, as well as provide walkable 3D interpretations of rooms from their houses using Three.js. In my essay linked in the site, I discuss the importance of advocacy for the preservation of such historical sites for educational and cultural purposes.
